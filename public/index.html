📱 How to Request Account Deletion

MsgSort offers two ways to request account and data deletion: directly inside the mobile app (recommended) or via this public web portal.

Method 1 (Instant in App)

Inside the MsgSort Android App

  • Open the MsgSort app on your Android device.
  • Tap the Profile tab on the bottom navigation bar.
  • Scroll down and select Delete Account & Data.
  • Review the erasure summary and tap Confirm Deletion.
Method 2 (Web Form / No App Access)

Via This Public Web Request Portal

  • Fill out the verified request form below.
  • Provide the registered Google Account email used for MsgSort.
  • Our support team validates ownership and executes deletion within the policy timeframe.

📊 Data Erasure & Retention Policy

Pursuant to Google Play Store User Data and Account Deletion policies, here is the transparent breakdown of what is erased, what may be temporarily retained, and the retention grounds:

Data Category Action Details & Legal Basis
User Authentication Profile Permanently Deleted Firebase Auth UID, registered email, display name, and login tokens are permanently erased.
Authored SortCodes & Messages Permanently Deleted All active, expired, and revoked SortCodes, text content, and metadata authored by your UID are deleted from Cloud Firestore.
Shared Files, Photos & PDFs Permanently Deleted All binaries, photos, videos, and PDF documents stored in Firebase Storage and Firestore Chunks are purged.
Financial & Billing Records Retained (Limited) Subscription order IDs and payment transaction IDs (Google Play Billing, BillDesk) are retained in anonymized accounting records for up to 180 days strictly to comply with tax laws, financial auditing, dispute resolution, and statutory fraud prevention.
Security & Firewall Logs Retained (Up to 30 Days) Ephemeral system logs (network timestamps, IP rate limit counters) are retained for up to 30 days to defend against distributed abuse and cyberattacks, after which they automatically rotate out.

⏱️ Expected Deletion Timeline

• In-App Deletions: Initiated immediately and finalized within 24 to 48 hours.
• Web Portal & Email Requests: Security review and owner identity verification is initiated within 48 hours. Once confirmed, all eligible account data and associated content is permanently purged within 7 to 30 calendar days.
